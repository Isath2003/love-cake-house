Our Story

Love Cake House is a home-based cake shop in Trincomalee, built with a big dream — to bring happiness through homemade cakes.

We believe that every celebration deserves a special cake. That's why we use fresh ingredients, simple recipes and a lot of love in every cake we bake.

From birthdays to anniversaries, we are here to make your special moments even sweeter!
